  limits every Hermes project on this machine shares. The owner is to say yes first. Command, when approved:

- No Docker container was started (stage B) and no model was called (stage C).

  work), every pinned model declares at least 64,000 tokens of context (the round 11 rule rejects none of them), the Lead and
  Reviewer are on different providers, and no provider key named in `config/models.yaml` is exported in the controller's shell.

  for all three roles (ASES-ROL-07), each role has toolsets it does not need (ASES-ROL-06; the Lead and coder-1 even have

  (ASES-ROL-03), and the kanban limits are unset or wrong (ASES-ROL-04, ROL-08, ARC-08, and `auto_decompose` is true, so Hermes
  could split a triage card into cards the plan does not know, ASES-ARC-02). Applying `swarm init --global` fixes all 22.
- Still open for the owner: `xkiro` and `opencode_free` have no source URL on record for their limits (ASES-VER-01); two
